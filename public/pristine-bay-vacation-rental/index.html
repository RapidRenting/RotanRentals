The setting

A quieter base for a full Roatán stay.

Pristine Bay is a coastal community on Roatán’s north shore. From the villa, guests can settle into private indoor-outdoor living while staying close to the Black Pearl Golf Course, the Pristine Bay shoreline and a nearby scuba shop.

The location works especially well for travellers who want activities within easy reach without giving up a calm home base. Return from a round of golf or a day on the water to your own pool, patios, kitchen and two complete king suites.

Golf woven into the landscape

The Black Pearl winds through Pristine Bay, putting the fairway close to the villa while preserving broad island and sea views.
